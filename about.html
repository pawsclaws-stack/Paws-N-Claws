Our Story

The All-In-One Solution for DC Pet Parents

We founded Paws N' Claws right here in Washington, DC to eliminate the hassle of managing multiple platforms, accounts, and independent providers just to keep your pets happy and your home running smoothly.

Dog Walking

Energetic neighborhood walks keeping your pups active, healthy, and happy around your neighborhood.

Non-Overnight Pet Sitting

Premium daytime drop-ins providing feeding, personalized playtime, and love in their familiar environment.

Light Housekeeping

Bringing in mail, watering plants, handling trash, and wiping down muddy paws so you return to a spotless home.

Native GPS Tracking. No Separate Accounts.

Most modern services force you to register for messy, third-party companion apps just to check on your dog's route. We engineered live **GPS tracking directly into our native platform**. Get real-time updates and maps seamlessly under your single, existing Paws N' Claws account.

Equipped for Everyday Emergencies

Safety is our absolute baseline on busy city streets. Every single walker handles assignments fully stocked with a portable backup kit containing extra collars, heavy-duty safety leashes, escape-proof harnesses, and plenty of emergency poop bags.

Proudly Serving Central DC

Our regular team coverage loops straight through the H Street Corridor, Florida Avenue NE, Chinatown, U Street, Capitol Hill, and Maryland Avenue NE to bring premium, bonded care directly to your block.
