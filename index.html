WELCOME TO THE PACK

Elevated Pet Care. Sparkling Homes. Total Peace of Mind.

At Paws N’ Claws, we believe premium pet care shouldn't come with compromise. We provide a luxury, all-in-one solution that seamlessly blends professional dog walking, cozy overnight pet sitting, and light housekeeping to keep both your furry companions happy and your living space sparkling clean. Engineered for the modern pet parent, our service goes far beyond the standard stroll; every visit is backed by live GPS tracking, instant photo updates, and a fully stocked toolkit of premium supplies brought right to your door. Whether we are burning off your pup’s energy on the trails or tidying up surface clutter before you arrive home, we treat your pack—and your home—like our absolute own.
